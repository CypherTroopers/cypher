Natural Earth's official terms identify its raster and vector map data as public
domain and permit modification, distribution, and personal, educational, and

earth-land.json derives only physical land outlines from that source. Coordinates
are simplified and rounded for a small offline globe, preserving every source
ring and its winding. See reports/earth-land-provenance.json for checksums,
coordinate schema, source version, and reproducible build details.

This asset contains no peer locations, IP addresses, or IP geolocation data.
